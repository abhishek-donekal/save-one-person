Our Mission

Save One Person

Save One Person is dedicated to helping connect people who need living kidney and liver donors with individuals willing to save a life.

We believe media can be one of the most powerful tools for creating miracles.

A single story can reach thousands of people.

One of those people may become the donor that changes a life forever.

"One story can save a life.
One donor can save a life.
One introduction can save a life."
What We Do

How We Create Miracles

Media Outreach

We share life-saving stories through television, radio, podcasts, newspapers, social media, and digital platforms.

Create Connections

We help create opportunities for people in need of living organ donors to be seen and heard.

Build Partnerships

We work with media companies, philanthropists, healthcare leaders, corporations, influencers, and community organizations.

Why This Matters

The Gap We Close

Many people in need of living organ donors are waiting, hoping, and praying for someone to step forward.

Many potential donors would gladly help if they simply heard the right story.

Save One Person exists to close that gap through media, technology, storytelling, and human connection.
