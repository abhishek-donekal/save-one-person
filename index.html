The Problem

Thousands Are Waiting.

Every day, individuals and families face an agonizing wait, not knowing if the call they are praying for will ever come.

Thousands of people urgently need living kidney donors, living liver donors, bone marrow matches, and other life-saving medical miracles. Many compassionate people would step forward if they simply knew someone needed them. The tragedy is not a shortage of willing hearts, but a shortage of visibility. Patients and potential donors occupy the same world but rarely cross paths.

Save One Person exists to close that gap. Through media, storytelling, and human connection, we bring the right story to the right person at the right time, because one introduction truly can save a life.

Our Mission

Media as a force for miracles.

We connect those who need living kidney donors, living liver donors, bone marrow matches, and other life-saving medical miracles with the individuals who have the power to save them. Behind every medical crisis is a human story, and that story, when amplified to the right audience, can unlock a life-changing response.

Our platform harnesses television, radio, podcasts, social media, and digital channels to broadcast these stories at scale. Because the most powerful tool in medicine is not always a drug or a procedure. Sometimes, it is simply putting two people in the same room.

One story can reach millions. One introduction can save a life. One act of generosity can change a family's future forever.
