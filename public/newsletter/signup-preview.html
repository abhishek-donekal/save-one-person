From: Lauren Finkelstein, Save One Person
Subject: Your free guide: How to Find a Living Kidney Donor

Hi friend, thank you for joining Save One Person. Here is your free guide…

How to Find a Living Kidney Donor

  1. Pick a Donor Champion to spread the word for you
  2. Tell your story: name, photo, what a new kidney would mean, your transplant center
  3. Spread the word everywhere: social media, place of worship, work, local TV, radio and newspapers
  4. Be ready for questions: who pays, travel and lost wages, paired exchange, changing your mind
  5. When someone says yes: connect them with the transplant center

Never offer or accept money for a kidney. It's illegal in the U.S.

Be the miracle, Lauren
