## Project title
South Florida Ready to Help: Finding Living Donors for Florida Patients

## Organization summary
Save One Person Inc. (EIN 84-4214447) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Sunny Isles Beach, Florida. We find living donors and medical miracles with the power of the media. We tell the stories of people who need a living kidney or liver donor or a bone marrow match, and put those stories in front of the people most likely to help. Our founder, Lauren Finkelstein, was named one of NYC's Top 50 Heroes by 1010 WINS. Our work has been covered by FOX 5, PIX11, NY1 and the Tony Robbins Podcast.

## Statement of need
Many patients with kidney failure can be saved by a living donor, but the hardest part is finding that person. Most families don't know how to ask the public for help, and most people have never been asked.

When people are asked, they respond. When FOX 5 aired our Save One Alert for Elaine DeLeon, a mom in a desperate search for a living kidney donor, hundreds of people called in wanting to help her, so many that the hospital had to turn people away. But until now, the people who weren't a match for that one patient had nowhere to go.

## Project description
With Wawa's support, we will run South Florida Ready to Help for 12 months:
1. **Florida patient stories.** Write and share the stories of Florida patients who need a living kidney or liver donor, through local TV, radio and newspapers, our weekly Save One Alert email, and social media.
2. **The Ready to Help list.** Launched November 2026 (saveonepersonnow.org/ready-to-help). People who step forward for one patient, but aren't a match, join the list, and we reach out to them when the next patient needs a donor. Joining never commits anyone to donate.
3. **Plain-language donor education.** Short videos and our "Myth vs Fact: Living Kidney Donation" page, so people understand what donating involves before they contact a transplant center.

All medical decisions, testing and matching are handled by transplant centers. Our role is awareness, storytelling and connection.

## Population served
Florida patients who need a living organ donor or bone marrow match, their families, and South Florida residents who could become donors.
