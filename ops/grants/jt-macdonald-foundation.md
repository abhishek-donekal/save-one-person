## Organization
Save One Person Inc. (EIN 84-4214447) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Sunny Isles Beach, Miami-Dade County. We find living donors and medical miracles with the power of the media, telling the stories of people who need a living kidney or liver donor or a bone marrow match so the right stranger can find them. Founder Lauren Finkelstein was named one of NYC's Top 50 Heroes by 1010 WINS; our work has been covered by FOX 5, PIX11, NY1 and the Tony Robbins Podcast.

## Request amount
$25,000 for a one-year Miami-Dade program.

## Need
Miami-Dade patients with kidney failure often wait years for a transplant. A living donor can shorten that wait, but most families don't know how to find one, and most residents have never been asked. When people are asked, they respond: when FOX 5 aired our alert for Elaine DeLeon, hundreds of people called in, so many that the hospital had to turn people away. The people who weren't her match had nowhere to go.

## Target population
Miami-Dade residents who need a living kidney or liver donor, their families, and Miami-Dade residents who could become donors, with Spanish-language materials for our Spanish-speaking neighbors.

## Project: Miami-Dade Ready to Help
1. Tell the stories of Miami-Dade patients who need a living donor, in English and Spanish, through local media, our Save One Alert email and social media.
2. Grow the Ready to Help list (saveonepersonnow.org/ready-to-help), so people who step forward for one patient can be contacted for the next.
3. Host plain-language living-donation talks with Miami-Dade congregations and community groups, in partnership with local transplant centers, which handle all medical decisions.
