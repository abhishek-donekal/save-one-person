### Our mission
Save One Person Inc. (EIN 84-4214447) is a 501(c)(3) public charity based in Sunny Isles Beach, Miami-Dade County. We find living donors and medical miracles with the power of the media. We tell the stories of people who need a living kidney donor, a living liver donor or a bone marrow match, and we put those stories on television, radio, podcasts, print and social media, where the right person is most likely to see them.

Our founder, Lauren Finkelstein, started Save One Person to close a gap she saw firsthand: there are people who would step forward to save a life if they only knew someone needed them. Lauren was named one of NYC's Top 50 Heroes by 1010 WINS, and our work has been covered by FOX 5, PIX11, NY1 and the Tony Robbins Podcast.

### The need
More than 94,000 people in the United States are waiting for a kidney transplant (HRSA/OPTN data, January 2026), and 17 people die every day waiting for a transplant. Many will wait years for a kidney from a deceased donor. A living donor can change that, but the hardest part for most families is the ask itself. Most patients don't know how to ask the public for help, and most people have never been asked.

When people are asked, they respond. When FOX 5 aired our Save One Alert for Elaine DeLeon, a mom searching for a living kidney donor, hundreds of people called in wanting to help, so many that the hospital had to turn people away. Until recently, the people who weren't a match for that one patient had nowhere to go.

### What we do today (programs)
1. **Save One Alerts and patient stories.** We write each patient's story with the family, point readers to the patient's own transplant center or registry, and share it through our media partners, our weekly Save One Alert email, our website and social media. Current stories include Florida patients such as 19-year-old Justin Lepri of St. Augustine, whose transplant team is at UF Health Shands.
2. **Media partnerships.** Segments such as PIX11's "Help Me Howard" have featured our patients, including Dr. Ron Richman's search for a living kidney donor and Rebecca Salame's search for a bone marrow match.
3. **The Ready to Help list** (saveonepersonnow.org/ready-to-help), launched November 2026. People who step forward for one patient but aren't a match join the list, and we contact them when the next patient needs a donor. Joining never commits anyone to donate.
4. **Plain-language education.** Website guides such as "How Living Kidney Donation Works" and "How to Find a Living Kidney Donor," and our "Myth vs Fact: Living Kidney Donation" page, so people understand what donating involves before they call a transplant center.

All testing, matching and medical decisions are made by transplant centers, never by Save One Person. Our role is awareness, storytelling and connection.
