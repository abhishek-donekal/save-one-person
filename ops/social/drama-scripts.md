# Save One Person: little drama scripts (DRAFT for Lauren)

Each is about 45 seconds, filmed vertically (phone held up and down). Fictional characters, clearly labeled.
On screen for the first 3 seconds and in the caption: **"Dramatization."**
Every one ends on the Save One Person heart logo + "Be the Miracle" + saveonepersonnow.org.

---

## 1. "One Post" (dad on dialysis, daughter shares his story)

**Cast:** Dad (50s), Daughter (20s), Stranger (30s, man or woman)

| Time | Picture | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Close-up: a hand taped to a dialysis line. | DAD (voice): "Three times a week. Four hours a chair." |
| 3–10 s | Dad in a recliner at home, tired. Daughter watches from the doorway. | DAUGHTER (to herself): "He'd never ask anyone." |
| 10–18 s | Daughter at the kitchen table on her phone, typing, deleting, typing. | DAUGHTER (reading as she types): "My dad needs a living kidney donor. I didn't know how to ask. So I'm asking you." She hits Post. |
| 18–26 s | Quick shots: the post shared again and again on different phones. | (No words. Soft music builds.) |
| 26–34 s | Stranger on a train, reading the post. Stops scrolling. Looks out the window. | STRANGER (quietly): "Blood type O." |
| 34–40 s | Daughter's phone rings. Unknown number. | STRANGER (voice on phone): "Hi. I saw your post about your dad." Daughter's hand goes to her mouth. |
| 40–45 s | Logo card. | Text: "One story. One stranger. One life. Be the Miracle. saveonepersonnow.org" |

## 2. "The Waiting Room"

**Cast:** Wife (40s), Woman (50s), Husband (40s, seen briefly)

| Time | Picture | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Hospital waiting room. Two women, two chairs apart. | WOMAN: "Is it your husband?" |
| 3–12 s | Wife nods, holding a coffee she isn't drinking. | WIFE: "He needs a living kidney donor. Nobody in the family is a match." |
| 12–18 s | Woman looks at her, then down at her own hands. | WOMAN: "What blood type is he?" WIFE (surprised): "B." |
| 18–24 s | Woman says nothing. Picks up her bag and leaves. Wife looks hurt. | (Silence.) |
| 24–34 s | Title: "6 months later." Same waiting room. Wife sits down. The same Woman walks in, wearing a hospital bracelet. | WIFE: "You..." WOMAN (smiling): "Turns out I'm a B too." |
| 34–40 s | Husband in a hospital bed, giving a weak thumbs up through a doorway. The two women hold hands. | WIFE (crying): "You don't even know us." WOMAN: "I do now." |
| 40–45 s | Logo card. | Text: "Strangers save lives every day. Be the Miracle. saveonepersonnow.org" |

## 3. "The Empty Chair" (Thanksgiving)

**Cast:** Grandmother (70s), Grandson (teen), Family at the table, Stranger (40s)

| Time | Picture | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Thanksgiving table, full of food. One empty chair with a name card: "Grandma." | GRANDSON (voice): "This is the first Thanksgiving she missed." |
| 3–10 s | Grandma at home in a robe, on a video call to the table, waving. | GRANDMA (on screen): "Save me some pie!" Everyone laughs, but it's sad. |
| 10–20 s | Grandson in his room, filming himself on his phone. | GRANDSON: "My grandma needs a living kidney donor. She taught me everything. Please share this." |
| 20–28 s | The video is shared again and again. A TV news anchor holds up a phone showing it. | ANCHOR (voice): "...a teenager's plea for his grandmother..." |
| 28–38 s | Title: "One year later." Same table. Grandma in her chair. Next to her, a new face: the Stranger. | GRANDMA: "Everyone, this is Michelle. She's family now." |
| 38–41 s | Stranger smiles; Grandson hands her the pie. | GRANDSON: "You get the first slice." |
| 41–45 s | Logo card. | Text: "What are you thankful for? Be the Miracle. saveonepersonnow.org" |

## How to make them

1. **Film them yourself with friends or family**, on a phone, vertical, in natural light. Real people get the most views. Each one needs only 2–3 people and 1–2 locations.
2. **Or use an AI video app** (ask your friend which one he uses). These scripts work as written; each scene is a short clip.

Once you have the clips, send them to me and I'll edit them: cut them tight, add the captions, the "Dramatization" label, the music and the logo ending.
