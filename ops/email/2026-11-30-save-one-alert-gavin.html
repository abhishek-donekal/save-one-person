HOW YOU CAN HELP GAVIN

1. Be his living kidney donor. Fill out Duke Health's free, confidential screening form. Donors must be 18+ with blood type B or O.

Get screened to be Gavin's donor

2. Not a match? You can still help Gavin by joining a kidney chain. Duke works with the National Kidney Registry. Through a paired exchange, you donate a kidney to someone who matches you, and Gavin gets a kidney from another donor who matches him. Use the same Duke form and say you want to donate on Gavin's behalf.

How kidney chains work

3. Reach Gavin's family on his COTA page: COTA.org/cotaforgavinsfight

4. Please forward this email to three people. One share could reach Gavin's donor. Read Gavin's story
