PERSON OF THE WEEK

Deomat, a 38-year-old dad of two, needs a living kidney donor

Deomat has fought polycystic kidney disease for more than 16 years. Today his kidneys work at less than 13%, and dialysis is coming soon. His greatest wish is simple: more birthdays with his children.

His family can't donate because the disease runs in the family, so they are asking kind strangers. Through the National Kidney Registry, you can help even if you're not his match.

🍋 THIS WEEK'S HEALTH HACK

Warm lemon water first thing in the morning

Before coffee, drink a glass of warm water with the juice of half a lemon. It's an easy way to start the day hydrated, and it gets you a little vitamin C too. Tip: use a straw and rinse with plain water after, to go easy on your teeth.
