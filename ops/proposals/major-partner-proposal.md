## 1. The opportunity

More than 100,000 people in the U.S. are waiting for an organ transplant, and most are waiting for a kidney. A living donor can end that wait, but only if the right person hears the story. Most living donors are not family members: they are people who saw, heard or read about someone who needed help.

Save One Person exists to make sure those stories are heard. A major partner would let us tell more of them, in more cities, every week.

## 2. What Save One Person does

Save One Person Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 84-4214447), founded by Lauren Finkelstein. It started in New York and Lauren is now based in Miami.

- **Save One Alerts.** We turn a patient's need into a media story: TV, radio, podcasts, social media and our weekly Save One Alert email, so the right donor can step forward.
- **Person of the Week.** Every week we feature people who need a living kidney or liver donor, a bone marrow match or another medical miracle (now: 3-year-old William Fritts, who needs a living kidney donor).
- **Urgent Requests.** Patients and families post their story on saveonepersonnow.org so donors and the public can reach them directly.
- **Education.** Living-donation facts, myth-vs-fact content and Lauren's Health Hacks videos.

Patient campaigns are only about finding a donor, never about money for the patient. Partner gifts support Save One Person the organization.
